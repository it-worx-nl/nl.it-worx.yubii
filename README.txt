Control the shutters of your Nice Yubii Home or Fibaro Home Center 3 directly from Homey — locally, no cloud.

Features
- Shutters and awnings: open, close and stop, with a position slider when the shutter reports its position
- Gates and garage doors (Nice): open, close and stop (beta)
- Lights and dimmers: on/off and dimming (beta)
- Switches and plugs, with power and energy when the hub measures it (beta)
- Sensors: motion, door/window, water, smoke, heat, CO, temperature, humidity, light, battery and tamper (beta)
- The hub is found automatically on your network
- State changes arrive within seconds
- Adjustable travel time and command repeat for shutters that sometimes miss the radio signal

"Beta" means: built according to the Fibaro HC3 API but not yet tested on every device. Something not working? Let us know via the support address.

Good to know
Nice one-way RF shutters do not report whether they are open or closed, so the app shows the last command sent.

This is an unofficial app; it is not made by Nice or Fibaro.
